The last season. Rahul now leads the Thursday group in his rented room and
walks with Arjun; the season ends, and the series with it, at the grave where
Season 1 ended. One real moment per reel, five frames a few seconds apart,
then the brand outro. Generate each frame with the previous frame attached.

Every prompt here follows the direction guide: each frame names its camera,
set, blocking and light; each clip names its transition type, one camera move,
three action beats, what must not change, and an ambient-only audio bed. Read
the **Staging** note at the top of each reel before generating — it is the map
the prompts are written against.

**Adults only.** Flow rejects anyone under 18, so every person in every frame is an adult.

**Rules that override composition** (from the scripts' production notes): no
office title, pulpit, stage, microphone, platform or clerical dress, and no
real preacher, church building, logo or channel — ever. Leaders are shown
from behind, as hands, or at a distance. Teaching is a circle of mats and a
printed Bible. No other faith's dress, mark or symbol. Never Jesus' face; in
a painting he is a figure from behind. Any on-screen words (a list, the
safety block, armour labels) are set as text in the edit, never generated
into the image. Frames of hands only are tagged hands-only, and "Rahul
matches the character reference" appears only where his face is visible.

Paintings: Jesus from behind over Jerusalem (3·3), the charcoal fire by the lake and the crowns laid down (3·4). The series ends where Season 1 ended: Rahul at the same grave, looking up.

Timing is unchanged: five clips of 10s, the last one a HOLD on frame 05 that
dissolves into your outro in the edit.

## REEL 3·1 — कोई वरदान कम नहीं था · S5·09
Sunday morning before the service, and the church is full of ordinary gifts: Arjun setting out floor mats, an older woman praying with a younger one in a corner, Rahul pouring water for the old people as they arrive, Joseph at the door. 1 Corinthians 13 open on a chair between it all. No stage, microphone, raised-hands crowd or glossolalia scene; nothing mocks either side.
**Cast:** Rahul, Arjun, Joseph, an older and a younger woman (one-off), congregation · **Hook text on frame 01:** "वरदान इतने — तो कमी क्या थी?"

**Staging.** The Sunday hall reference. The camera starts **at the front wall
looking back down the hall toward the open double door**; the windows and the
morning sun are on the screen-right wall, the three plastic chairs against the
screen-left wall a third of the way down. Anyone seated on the mats faces the
front wall — **toward the lens** — but nobody looks into it. The two women sit
on the first two chairs, **the older woman screen-left, the younger screen-right**,
turned toward each other; the open Bible lies on the third chair, screen-right
of the younger woman. Rahul works at the open door at the far end, on its
screen-right side; the arriving elderly couple sit just inside it on its
screen-left side. The camera pans left to the women, tilts down to the Bible,
looks up the hall to the door, then pulls back to where it began.
