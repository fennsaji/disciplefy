# Season 5 · Arc 3 — Instagram captions

Short by design: hook, one line, verse, CTA, five hashtags (Instagram's cap).
Hook and closing line are lifted from the reviewed scripts, with each reel's
caption rules. 3·1 carries the three *आज भी हैं?* lines verbatim and has no
gift quiz or *comment your gift*. 3·2 has no battle, attack, chain or
deliverance language and no warfare hashtag. 3·3 carries the cross line and
the conditional, never quotes मत्ती 25:46 alone, and has no fire emoji or
*share this with someone* prompt. 3·4, the series finale, carries no
subscription, trial, plan or download prompt — only *save the series*. Pins
follow the notes (the finale pins *John 10* first; *1 Peter 5* waits until
its Hindi description is fixed).
