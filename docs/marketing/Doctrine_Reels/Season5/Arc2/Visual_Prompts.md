The last season. Rahul now leads the Thursday group in his rented room and
walks with Arjun; the season ends, and the series with it, at the grave where
Season 1 ended. One real moment per reel, five frames a few seconds apart,
then the brand outro. Generate each frame with the previous frame attached.

Every prompt here follows the direction guide: each frame names its camera,
set, blocking and light; each clip names its transition type, one camera move,
three action beats, what must not change, and an ambient-only audio bed. Read
the **Staging** note at the top of each reel before generating — it is the map
the prompts are written against.

**Adults only.** Flow rejects anyone under 18, so every person in every frame is an adult.

**Rules that override composition** (from the scripts' production notes): no
office title, pulpit, stage, microphone, platform or clerical dress, and no
real preacher, church building, logo or channel — ever. Leaders are shown
from behind, as hands, or at a distance. Teaching is a circle of mats and a
printed Bible. No other faith's dress, mark or symbol. Never Jesus' face; in
a painting he is a figure from behind. Any on-screen words (a list, the
safety block, armour labels) are set as text in the edit, never generated
into the image. Frames of hands only are tagged hands-only, and "Rahul
matches the character reference" appears only where his face is visible.

Paintings: Paul dictating the letter (2·1), the basin and towel — hands and feet only (2·2), the scattered sheep (2·3), Paul and Peter in the courtyard (2·4). 2·3 has no person until its last frame, and no abuse is shown or implied anywhere.

Timing is unchanged: five clips of 10s, the last one a HOLD on frame 05 that
dissolves into your outro in the edit.

## REEL 2·1 — सूची में एक ही हुनर · S5·05
Rahul has been asked to lead the Thursday group. At his desk he reads the list Paul wrote — one skill, the rest character. Paul dictating the letter in the painting; the uncle's lit doorway at dusk, seen from the lane; Rahul listening to his group; and Rahul on his knees at night. No office titles, no dress, no stage.
**Cast:** Rahul, his small group (Arjun, Joseph, others) · the uncle's home seen only from outside · painted scene (Paul dictating to a scribe, from behind) · **Hook text on frame 01:** "उस सूची में न मीठी आवाज़ है, न भीड़, न चमत्कार।"

**Staging.** Rahul's rented room from the reference. At the desk, Rahul sits
facing the screen-right wall; the camera is **between the desk and the wall,
high, looking down and back over the top of the open Bible**, so the page faces
Rahul and his face is cropped out above the frame. For the group and the
prayer, the camera stands **at the doorway, seated eye level, looking across
the room to the curtained window**: the bed is along the screen-left wall, the
desk and lamp on the screen-right wall. In the group, **Arjun sits on the
screen-left side of the circle, Rahul at the far side just screen-right of
centre facing toward the doorway, Joseph on the screen-right side**; two
members sit on the near side with their backs to camera. Everyone faces the
centre of the circle. The lane is a separate place: the camera stands on the
opposite side of the lane looking straight at the lit doorway.
