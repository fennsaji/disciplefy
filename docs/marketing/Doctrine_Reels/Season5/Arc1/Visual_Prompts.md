The last season. Rahul now leads the Thursday group in his rented room and
walks with Arjun; the season ends, and the series with it, at the grave where
Season 1 ended. One real moment per reel, five frames a few seconds apart,
then the brand outro. Generate each frame with the previous frame attached.
Five clips of 10s, the last carrying frame 05 into your outro.

**Rules that override composition** (from the scripts' production notes): no
office title, pulpit, stage, microphone, platform or clerical dress, and no
real preacher, church building, logo or channel — ever. Leaders are shown
from behind, as hands, or at a distance. Teaching is a circle of mats and a
printed Bible. No other faith's dress, mark or symbol. Never Jesus' face; in
a painting he is a figure from behind. Any on-screen words (a list, the
safety block, armour labels) are set as text in the edit, never generated
into the image. Frames of hands only are tagged hands-only.

Paintings: Ezra over the scroll (1·1), the Levites explaining the law in small groups — no platform (1·2), Paul's last letter tied with a cord (1·4). Rahul now leads the Thursday group; the teaching is always a circle of mats and a printed Bible.
