# Season 5 · Arc 1 — सिखाने वाला · Hindi voiceover scripts (reels 1·1–1·4)

Curriculum: the *Series roadmap* section of
[../../Curriculum.md](../../Curriculum.md), Stage 5 *चरवाहा — the shepherd*,
Arc 1 *सिखाने वाला (the one who teaches)*. Season 5 has no curriculum page of
its own yet; the stage intro and the roadmap's per-reel *must land / must
avoid* table are the brief, and Season 1's principles carry over whole.

The viewer has been asked to lead a group or to teach. **Arc 1 precedes Arc 2
because a leader who has not learned to teach the text will lead by
personality.** This arc therefore stays with the *text* and the teacher's
relation to it — the warning (S5·01), the method (S5·02), the limit (S5·03)
and the guard (S5·04) — and says nothing about office, structure or who
appoints whom. Leadership character and servant leadership are Arc 2's; no
reel here pre-empts them.

**What this arc builds on, and does not repeat.** Season 4 Arc 1 taught the
disciple to *read* — five questions any reader asks of his own Bible
(*कहानी में कहाँ? / कैसी किताब? / ऊपर-नीचे? / किससे कहा गया? / मसीह ने इसके
साथ क्या किया?*). This arc turns the same reader round to face a room: none
of those questions is re-taught, and none of S4·01–05's texts (लूका 24:27,
नीतिवचन 22:6, 26:4-5, यिर्मयाह 29:11, फिलिप्पियों 4:13, यहोशू 1, मत्ती 5:17)
is voiced. S2·13 taught the *listener* to test the one who teaches him
(प्रेरितों 17:11, 1 तीमुथियुस 2:5, मत्ती 23:8-10), and showed याकूब 3:1 on
screen, unread; this arc is written from **the other side of the Bible** —
the teacher tested by the same word — and does not re-voice the Bereans, the
one mediator, or the गुरु saying. Season 1 reel 13 voiced गलातियों 1:8's
*angel* clause and S2·03 voiced *दूसरा सुसमाचार*; S5·04 uses गलातियों 1:7
(*no gospel at all*) and 1:8's *we* clause only. S2·09 gave the gospel test
for a *church*; S5·04 asks it of a *teaching*, in three questions, and does
not repeat that reel's wording.

**The arc's guard against its own biggest risk:** a teaching arc can make
teaching sound like a guild's craft, or make the teacher a watchman whose
job is to find heretics. So **every test in this arc is turned first on the
viewer's own teaching** — S5·02's *remove the verse* is run on *आपका पिछला
पाठ*, never on a sermon he heard; S5·04's guarding begins with *पहले अपनी…
चौकसी* — and **no reel names, pictures or implies a preacher, church,
denomination, channel or movement as the false teacher or the bad teacher**.
Every error enters as something the viewer might do himself.

**v2: simplified** — a one-line hook first, wording a 15-year-old follows, one idea per reel, about 50 seconds; each reel's *Guardrails held* note lists what was cut. Rules: *Script rules* in [Season2/Curriculum.md](../../Season2/Curriculum.md).

Four new reels, production IDs `S5·01`–`S5·04` (Season 5 is 4 / 4 / 4; Arc 2
will be S5·05–08, Arc 3 S5·09–12), in arc order. Arc position is the
heading; the production ID stays in the `===` block. All four hold आप
register, no English loanwords, same voice as Seasons 1–4. Honorifics: यीशु
and पवित्र आत्मा take **वे**; परमेश्वर in general takes **वह**; पौलुस,
याकूब, पतरस *लिखते/कहते हैं*; एज्रा takes the honorific verb (*चले*).
Scripture is paraphrased in indirect speech; nothing is placed in quotation
marks unless the OV wording is certain (the hook is in quotes by format; its
Scripture is reported speech). तू/कर inside Scripture's reported speech is not
a register slip. Church leaders, where mentioned, are **अगुवे**; no office
title is used, and *उपदेशक* (याकूब 3:1's OV word) is not voiced — the reels
say *सिखाने वाला*, a function, not a rank. Every reel stands alone — the
algorithm serves any one of them cold — and restates in one line whatever it
leans on. The final sentence of each script begins at roughly 0:40 and lands
over the brand outro. Length is measured on the Devanagari-token counter
(145–152 = 48–50s); all four land 150–152.

## REEL 1·1 — S5·01 · हाँ कहें, या ना?

```
=== DOCTRINE REELS · S5·01 · हाँ कहें, या ना? ===
Target: about 50 seconds, unhurried. Register: आप. Tone: warm and a little uncertain at the open, as someone just asked; the याकूब lines level, never stern; *मनाही नहीं — होश दिलाना है* with lift; the conditional line plain; the closing two short beats, evenly weighted.

"इस बार बाइबल अध्ययन आप लीजिए।"
और तभी एक आयत याद आई — बहुत लोग सिखाने वाले न बनें।
तो हाँ कहें, या ना?

याकूब कारण भी लिखते हैं — सिखाने वालों का हिसाब और सख़्त होगा।
क्यों? क्योंकि जो वचन सिखाता है, लोग उसकी बात पर जीते हैं।

पर यह मनाही नहीं — होश दिलाना है। वही बाइबल कहती है — एक-दूसरे को सिखाओ।
और यह चेतावनी किसी ख़ास वर्ग की नहीं — बच्चों को वचन सिखाने वाले की भी है। सिखाना कोई ऊँचा दर्जा भी नहीं।

एज्रा ने मन लगाया — वचन को समझने, उस पर चलने, फिर सिखाने में।
और याकूब मानते हैं — हम सब बहुत बार चूकते हैं।
अगर आप मसीह में हैं — यह हिसाब उद्धार का नहीं; जो सिखाया, उसका है।

डर से ना मत कहिए। घमंड से हाँ मत कहिए।

=== END ===
```

**Length:** ~130 words · about 50 seconds at an unhurried pace. The blank lines mark the five 10-second clips. Editor: **never** cut *"पर यह मनाही नहीं — होश दिलाना है"* with the *एक-दूसरे को सिखाओ* line after it (one unit), *"यह चेतावनी किसी ख़ास वर्ग की नहीं…"* with *"सिखाना कोई ऊँचा दर्जा भी नहीं"*, *"अगर आप मसीह में हैं — यह हिसाब उद्धार का नहीं; जो सिखाया, उसका है"* or the closing pair (one unit; either half alone is the error the reel refuses). If the artist runs long, cut *"और याकूब मानते हैं — हम सब बहुत बार चूकते हैं।"*

**On-screen Scripture**

| Line | Reference |
|---|---|
| "बहुत लोग सिखाने वाले न बनें" | याकूब 3:1 |
| "हिसाब और सख़्त होगा" | याकूब 3:1 — hold |
| "एक-दूसरे को सिखाओ" | कुलुस्सियों 3:16 |
| "कोई ऊँचा दर्जा भी नहीं" | मत्ती 23:8 — shown, not read |
| "समझने… चलने… सिखाने" | एज्रा 7:10 |
| "हम सब बहुत बार चूकते हैं" | याकूब 3:2 |
| "हिसाब उद्धार का नहीं" | 1 कुरिन्थियों 3:12-15 |
| closing line | 1 पतरस 4:10-11 |

**Why the hook works:** the request itself, as it is made in every small church, and the verse that jumps to mind to refuse it. The viewer has been in that second and said either yes too fast or no too fast.

**Guardrails held:** **याकूब 3:1 is a warning to enter soberly, not a ban** — said in so many words and backed by the New Testament's own *teach one another*. **No clericalism**: the warning reaches the humblest teaching, and teaching is no higher rank. **The stricter account is not about salvation**, under the verbatim conditional. Ezra set his heart to study, to do and then to teach (एज्रा 7:10) — the positive pattern, said as the verse says it. **The closing refuses both errors at once** and is one unit. Cut from v1: *और वे 'हम' लिखते हैं — अपने को भी गिनते हैं*; *बच्चों की कक्षा में सिखाने वाले* is now *बच्चों को वचन सिखाने वाले*.

## REEL 1·2 — S5·02 · आयत हटाकर देखिए

```
=== DOCTRINE REELS · S5·02 · आयत हटाकर देखिए ===
Target: about 50 seconds, unhurried. Register: आप. Tone: friendly and a little wry at the open — a test the viewer runs on himself, never on anyone else; the नहेम्याह beats slow; the *कहानी, उदाहरण — अच्छे हैं* line reassuring; the closing with a small smile.

अपना पिछला पाठ याद कीजिए।
अगर उसमें से बाइबल की आयत हटा दें — तो क्या आपकी बात वही रहती?
अगर हाँ — तो आयत बस एक खूँटी थी। बात आपकी थी; उस पर टाँग दी।

नहेम्याह के समय में परमेश्वर की पुस्तक पढ़ी गई, अर्थ समझाया गया — और लोग समझ गए।

पौलुस ने तीमुथियुस से कहा — वचन का प्रचार कर।
तो उस हिस्से की मुख्य बात ही आपके पाठ की मुख्य बात हो।
हर शब्द में छिपा अर्थ मत ढूँढिए — जो लिखने वाला कहना चाहता था, वही।

हिम्मत बढ़ाने वाली अपनी बातों में आयत जोड़ देने से वे वचन नहीं बन जातीं।
कहानी, उदाहरण — अच्छे हैं, अगर वे आयत की बात खोलें।
परमेश्वर का वादा है — उसका वचन व्यर्थ नहीं लौटता। यह वादा वचन के लिए है — आपके विचार के लिए नहीं।

आयत हटाइए। अगर पूरा पाठ गिर जाए — तब आपने वचन सिखाया।

=== END ===
```

**Length:** ~142 words · about 50 seconds at an unhurried pace. The blank lines mark the five 10-second clips. Editor: **never** cut *"तो उस हिस्से की मुख्य बात ही आपके पाठ की मुख्य बात हो"*, *"कहानी, उदाहरण — अच्छे हैं, अगर वे आयत की बात खोलें"*, *"यह वादा वचन के लिए है — आपके विचार के लिए नहीं"* or the closing line. If the artist runs long, cut *"हर शब्द में छिपा अर्थ मत ढूँढिए —"* only together with its second half.

**On-screen Scripture**

| Line | Reference |
|---|---|
| "पढ़ी गई… अर्थ समझाया गया… लोग समझ गए" | नहेम्याह 8:8 |
| "वचन का प्रचार कर" | 2 तीमुथियुस 4:2 |
| "जो लिखने वाला कहना चाहता था" | 2 कुरिन्थियों 1:13 |
| "वचन व्यर्थ नहीं लौटता" | यशायाह 55:11 |
| closing line | 2 तीमुथियुस 2:15 |

**Why the hook works:** a test the viewer can run on himself in three seconds, on a lesson he actually taught. Most teachers find the answer is *yes, my point would have stood* — and stay to hear why that matters.

**Guardrails held:** **The passage's point is the lesson's point** — the reel's one rule, taken from नहेम्याह 8:8's own three verbs. **Allegory is refused** (no hidden meaning in every word; the author's intent). **Motivational talk with a verse attached is named for what it is**, without mocking anyone. **Stories and illustrations are affirmed** when they open the text. **The promise that the word does not return empty belongs to the word, not to the teacher's thought.** Cut from v1: *क्योंकि दिल वचन से बदलते हैं*, *आगे बढ़ने के नुस्ख़े*.

## REEL 1·3 — S5·03 · मुझे नहीं पता

```
=== DOCTRINE REELS · S5·03 · मुझे नहीं पता ===
Target: about 50 seconds, unhurried. Register: आप. Tone: sympathetic at the open, the room's silence felt; the पतरस line with a little relief; *बात गढ़ने में है* firm; the आत्मा lines respectful, no irony on the quoted phrase; the closing warm.

आप बाइबल अध्ययन ले रहे हैं। किसी ने सवाल पूछा — और आपको जवाब नहीं पता।
सब आपकी ओर देख रहे हैं। अब?

पतरस ने पौलुस की चिट्ठियों के बारे में लिखा — उनमें कुछ बातें समझने में कठिन हैं।
और ख़तरा बताया — कुछ लोग ऐसी बातों को खींच-तानकर अपना ही नाश करते हैं।
तो ख़तरा 'नहीं पता' कहने में नहीं। बात गढ़ने में है।

मूसा ने कहा — गुप्त बातें परमेश्वर की हैं; जो प्रगट हुईं, वे हमारी हैं — मानने के लिए।
तो सच कहिए — मुझे नहीं पता। साथ ढूँढेंगे।

साफ़ आयतों की रोशनी में कठिन आयत पढ़िए — वचन अपने को नहीं काटता।
और 'आत्मा ने मुझे दिखाया' कहकर नया अर्थ मत जोड़िए। पवित्र आत्मा समझ देते हैं — जो लिखा है, उसी की।
जो सबसे ज़रूरी है — सुसमाचार — वह धुंधला नहीं।

'मुझे नहीं पता' — सिखाने वाले की हार नहीं। वचन का आदर है।

=== END ===
```

If the artist runs long, cut *"सब आपकी ओर देख रहे हैं।"*

**On-screen Scripture**

|---|---|
| "समझने में कठिन" | 2 पतरस 3:15-16 |
| "गुप्त बातें परमेश्वर की हैं" | व्यवस्थाविवरण 29:29 |

**Why the hook works:** the moment every new teacher dreads, told in the second person, ending on one word — *अब?* The viewer wants to know what to do before it happens to him.

**Guardrails held:** **An apostle says an apostle is hard to understand** — so *I don't know* is in good company. **The danger is inventing, not admitting.** व्यवस्थाविवरण 29:29 gives the boundary: some things are God's. **Scripture interprets Scripture** — the clear lights the unclear. **Mysticism is refused and the Spirit is honoured in one unit**: he gives understanding of what is written, not new meanings. **The gospel is not among the unclear things.** Cut from v1: *एक प्रेरित — दूसरे प्रेरित के बारे में*, *अनसीखे लोग*.

## REEL 1·4 — S5·04 · सौंपी हुई अमानत

```
=== DOCTRINE REELS · S5·04 · सौंपी हुई अमानत ===
Target: about 50 seconds, unhurried. Register: आप. Tone: grave and quiet at the open, a last letter; the गलातिया lines plain, like a Bible study; the three questions slow and separated; *पहरेदारी का शौक़ नहीं* level, never sarcastic; the closing gentle, a hand passing something on.

पौलुस ने अपनी आख़िरी चिट्ठी में एक चीज़ की रखवाली करने को कहा।
सोना नहीं। इमारत नहीं।
सुसमाचार — जो एक ही बार सौंपा गया। न जोड़ना, न घटाना।

गलातिया में कोई यीशु को नकार नहीं रहा था। बस जोड़ रहे थे — मसीह, और व्यवस्था के काम।
पौलुस ने कहा — यह कोई सुसमाचार नहीं।
परख आदमी से नहीं, बात से — चाहे हम ही क्यों न हों।

परखने के तीन सवाल —
यीशु कौन हैं? क्रूस ने क्या किया, और क्या वे जी उठे? उद्धार — दान है, या कमाई?

पर रखवाली पहरेदारी का शौक़ नहीं। पौलुस कहते हैं — पहले अपनी, और अपनी शिक्षा की चौकसी।
नाम गिनाना नहीं — सच साफ़ सिखाना।
और यह अपने बल से नहीं — पवित्र आत्मा के द्वारा, जो हम में रहते हैं।

अमानत आपकी नहीं। जैसी मिली, वैसी ही आगे सौंपिए।

=== END ===
```

If the artist runs long, cut *"सोना नहीं। इमारत नहीं।"*

| "एक ही बार सौंपा गया" | यहूदा 3 |
| "यह कोई सुसमाचार नहीं" | गलातियों 1:6-9 · 2:16 |

| closing line | 2 तीमुथियुस 2:2 |

**Why the hook works:** an old man's last letter and the one thing he asks to be guarded — with two guesses ruled out before the answer. It tells a leader what his real property is.

**Guardrails held:** **The deposit is the gospel, and the Galatian error was addition, not denial** — which is how it usually comes. **Teaching is tested by content, not by who says it — *ourselves included*.** The three questions are the test, whole. **Guarding is not heresy-hunting**: first one's own teaching, no naming of names, and by the Spirit, not in one's own strength. The closing makes the leader a trustee. Cut from v1: *गीत का ढंग, अंत के दिनों का क्रम — इन पर भाई अलग सोचते हैं* — the secondary-matters point is S5·08's whole subject four reels on and is not said twice in the season; *बिगाड़ है*.

## Production notes

**Release in arc order:** S5·01 → S5·02 → S5·03 → S5·04. Two orderings are
hard. **S5·02 precedes S5·03** — the *I don't know* reel assumes a teacher
who is teaching the passage; released first, *don't invent* arrives before
*teach the text* and is heard as *don't teach*. **S5·04 closes the arc** —
guarding the gospel is the reel most easily turned into heresy-hunting, and
it needs S5·01's *हम सब बहुत बार चूकते हैं* and S5·03's *मुझे नहीं पता* behind
it so the guard is a humble teacher, not a watchman; if S5·04 slips, end the
run on S5·02, never on S5·01 (which ends on a choice, not on the word). Every
reel still stands alone; the order protects only the playlist viewer.

**Cross-season dependencies.** **Season 4 Arc 1 must be live** — this arc
assumes the reader's five questions and never re-teaches them; S5·02's
*मुख्य बात* is S4·03's paragraph question turned toward a room. **S5·01
after S2·13** (the listener's side; S5·01 voices याकूब 3:1, which S2·13
showed unread, and shows मत्ती 23:8, which S2·13 also only shows) and **after Season 1
reel 15/16** (the *हिसाब उद्धार का नहीं* line leans on justification being
settled). **S5·03 after Season 1 reel 13** (reel 13 tested impressions by
the word; S5·03 applies the same rule to a teacher's *'आत्मा ने मुझे
दिखाया'* and must not re-argue it). **S5·04 after Season 1 reel 13, S2·03,
S2·09 and S4·06** — it takes the Galatians verdict, the church test and the
one-breath gospel as given and must never be edited to re-voice गलातियों
1:8's angel clause or *दूसरा सुसमाचार*. **Forward:** Arc 2·2 (*सेवक*, the
spiral of S2·13) must not contradict S5·01's *सिखाना कोई ऊँचा दर्जा भी नहीं*;
Arc 2·4 (*ज़रूरी और गौण*) will teach essentials and secondary matters at
length. In v2 S5·04 no longer voices its one secondary-matters line — the
season says that once, in Arc 2·4 — and S5·04's three questions confine its
test to gospel content. Arc 3·1 (spiritual gifts) must not undo S5·03's refusal to adjudicate
cessationism.

**Visual rules.**
- **No pulpit, stage, microphone, platform, robe, collar, stole or any
  clerical dress** in any of the four reels — the teaching shown is a small
  circle of chairs or floor mats, a printed Bible, a notebook. The arc names
  no office and its visuals may not supply one.
- **No footage, still or likeness of any real preacher, church building,
  signboard, logo, channel, stage event or crowd** — not even blurred. No
  screen showing a sermon or a video.
- **S5·01**: no child's face in distress, no scolding adult at the
  *बच्चों को वचन सिखाने वाले* beat — a child's hand turning a page at most. No judge,
  gavel, scales or ledger at *हिसाब* (the reel refused the ledger frame for
  sin; it keeps it off teaching too).
- **S5·02**: the hook's test may be pictured literally — a verse card lifted
  off a lesson page, and at the closing the page folding without it. No
  motivational-speaker imagery (arms raised on a stage, a crowd, a sunrise
  with a slogan) at *हिम्मत की बातें* — the beat is a notebook of bullet
  points with a verse clipped to the top.
- **S5·03**: the silence is a small group looking at the teacher, no one
  mocking; nothing that pictures the Spirit (no dove, light-beam or glow) at
  *पवित्र आत्मा समझ देते हैं* — the visual stays on the open page.
- **S5·04**: the *अमानत* is a cloth-wrapped bundle or a folded letter handed
  from one pair of hands to another — **no gold or jewellery** (the hook says
  *सोना नहीं*), no church building (*इमारत नहीं*). **No knife, no body, no
  ritual imagery at the Galatians beat**; the visual is a page with a line
  being written *onto* it in a second hand. No warning signs, red crosses
  over faces, magnifying glasses, watchtowers or *wanted* imagery at
  *पहरेदारी* — the refusal is voiced over a teacher reading alone.
- Frames that show only hands on a Bible are not tagged "matches the
  character reference".

**Comment moderation.**
- **Arc-wide:** **any comment that names or identifies a preacher, church,
  denomination, ministry, channel or movement — as a false teacher, a bad
  teacher, or an example of anything in these reels — is hidden and never
  answered in public**, whether the naming is hostile or admiring. No reply
  ever confirms or denies that a named person or group is sound. Comments
  asking *is X a false teacher?* get S5·04's three questions and a pointer to
  the viewer's own अगुवे, and nothing more.
- **S5·01:** **comments on who may teach — women teaching, age, ordination,
  training, a certificate — are not engaged**; these are polity and
  secondary questions outside the series. The reply is the reel's *यह
  चेतावनी किसी ख़ास वर्ग की नहीं* and the viewer's own कलीसिया's अगुवे.
  Comments announcing *I'll start my own group* get *एज्रा का क्रम* and the
  same pointer — the hook has the viewer *asked* by his church, and moderation
  keeps it there. Comments from teachers afraid they will lose their
  salvation get the conditional line verbatim, never *don't worry*.
- **S5·02:** comments quoting or describing a sermon (*our pastor always
  does this*) are not agreed with or corrected; moderation gives the hook's
  test turned on oneself (*अपना पिछला पाठ*) and nothing more. Comments
  defending allegory or a favourite spiritualised reading get S4·01's rule
  (*जहाँ वचन खुद जोड़ता है — वहीं जोड़िए*) and no ridicule.
- **S5·03:** hard questions posted in the comments (*what is the unforgivable
  sin?*, *explain Revelation 13*) are **not
  answered in the thread** — the reel's own line (*साथ ढूँढेंगे*) and a
  pointer to the viewer's church. Comments saying *the Spirit told me what
  this means* are met with the paired आत्मा lines, never mocked, and no
  cessationism or continuation argument is entered.
- **S5·04 is the arc's comment hazard.** Expect lists of names. Hide them.
  Comments treating a secondary matter as the gospel (a day of worship, a
  baptism mode, a rapture timing, a song style) get the reel's *ये सुसमाचार
  नहीं* line and are not argued. Comments about circumcision, another
  religion, or *the law* as a community marker are not engaged. No reply
  lists *signs*, *markers* or *red flags*.
