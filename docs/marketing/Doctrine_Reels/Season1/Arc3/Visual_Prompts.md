Same concept: one person, one real moment, the whole reel inside it. Rahul is
the thread. Amit, Sunil and the uncle from Reel 3 all return; two new faces are
referenced below. Generate each frame with the previous frame attached.

Every prompt here follows the direction guide: each frame names its camera,
set, blocking and light; each clip names its transition type, one camera move,
three action beats, what must not change, and an ambient-only audio bed. Read
the **Staging** note at the top of each reel before generating — it is the map
the prompts are written against.

**Adults only.** Flow rejects anyone under 18, so every person in every frame is an adult.

The painting device appears three times in nine reels, only where the
voiceover tells a Bible event: Jesus' baptism (Reel 12), Gethsemane (Reel 14),
the ascension (Reel 19). Jesus is seen from behind or as a distant figure in
light — never a face.

Timing is unchanged: five clips of 10s, the last one a HOLD on frame 05 that
dissolves into your outro in the edit, the closing line landing over the brand
frame.

## REEL 11 — नया जन्म और पवित्र आत्मा
A prayer meeting where people around Rahul are visibly moved and he feels nothing. He slips out onto the steps convinced he has been left outside — and opens Romans 8 to find he never was.
**Cast:** Rahul, a congregation (background) · **Hook text on frame 01:** "और आप अब भी बाहर खड़े हैं?"

**Staging.** The church hall reference. The stage is at one end, the open
double door at the other. Inside the hall the camera stands **at the stage
edge looking back toward the door**, so every worshipper faces the stage —
which means they face the lens — and the open door is behind them. Rahul stands
in the second row, just screen-right of the aisle. Outside, the camera stands
at the foot of the steps looking up at the door; through it you see the
worshippers' **backs**, because they still face the stage.
