# Arc 2 — Instagram captions

Short by design: hook, one line, verse, CTA, five hashtags (Instagram's cap).
Hook and closing line are lifted from the reviewed scripts. Pins from the
script's production notes: reel 8 under reel 7 (reel 5 as a second pin); reel
7 under reel 10; reel 5 under Arc 1 reel 4 once live.
