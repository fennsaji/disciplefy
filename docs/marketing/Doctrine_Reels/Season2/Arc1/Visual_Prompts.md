Same concept, a year on: one person, one real moment, the whole reel inside it.
Seven reels predate the season's re-cut; only their arc number changed and
nothing is recorded. Generate each frame with the previous frame attached.

Every prompt here follows the direction guide: each frame names its camera,
set, blocking and light; each clip names its transition type, one camera move,
three action beats, what must not change, and an ambient-only audio bed. Read
the **Staging** note at the top of each reel before generating — it is the map
the prompts are written against.

**Adults only.** Flow rejects anyone under 18, so every person in every frame is an adult.

**Safety rules that override composition** (from the curriculum's risk
register): no shrine, deity image or puja corner in any frame; nothing framed
for contrast with another faith; no antagonist, crowd or confrontation in any
reel about pressure at home — the only people on screen are the ones the reel
tells the viewer to go to.

The painting appears once, in Reel 1·2 — the Ethiopian official reading Isaiah, the one Bible event this arc narrates. Vikram carries three of the five reels.

Timing: five clips of 10s, the last one a HOLD on frame 05 that dissolves into
your outro in the edit, the closing line landing over the brand frame.

## REEL 1·1 — तीन भगवान? · S2·01
Evening chai on the rooftop of Rahul's building. Vikram, the neighbour from the floor below, asks the question he has been sitting on for months — three gods? Rahul answers without a diagram, and the chai does not go cold.
**Cast:** Rahul, Vikram · **Hook text on frame 01:** "तो क्या वे खुद से बात कर रहे थे?"

**Staging.** One flat rooftop, one camera axis for the whole reel. The camera
stands on the roof **with its back to the stairwell door**, looking across the
roof toward the waist-high parapet and the city beyond it. The stairwell door's
bulb is behind the camera and becomes the key light once it is dark. The two men
lean sideways on the parapet facing each other in profile, the city lights
behind them: **Vikram is always screen-left, Rahul screen-right.** In the last
frame they turn to lean on the parapet and look out at the city, so their backs
are to the camera.
