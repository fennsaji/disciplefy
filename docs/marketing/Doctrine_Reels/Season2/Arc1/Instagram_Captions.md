Hook and closing line are lifted from the reviewed scripts. Pins from the
script's production notes: S2·2 live before S2·5 and pinned under it with the
reel's own *कलीसिया के परिपक्व लोगों से बात कीजिए* line — nothing else. No
payment handle or fundraiser anywhere near S2·6.
