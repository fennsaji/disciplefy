# Doctrine Reels — Curriculum

Short-form reels (~45–60s) taking a Hindi-speaking Indian viewer from **new
believer to disciple**. Doctrinal review and sequencing by the
`paul-the-apostle` agent; app-alignment claims verified against
`backend/supabase/migrations/`.

Status: **planning**. No scripts written yet.

---

## Two constraints that shape everything

### 1. Reels do not deliver sequence

A curriculum assumes order. Instagram and YouTube Shorts deliver whichever reel
the algorithm surfaces, to a viewer who has seen none of the others.

So the series is built twice at once:

- **Ordered for the playlist viewer**, where dependencies are real.
- **Safe in isolation for the drive-by viewer**, where every reel will be
  someone's first and only.

Reels dangerous out of order must be self-limiting **on the face of the reel** —
a conditional clause, a one-line backward reference. Release order alone does
not protect a doctrine.

### 2. New believer → disciple: person before proposition

The opening reels meet a new believer where they are, not where a systematics
syllabus starts. Prolegomena-first is how a theology textbook is built; it is
not how the apostles preached. Acts 2 and Acts 10 open on the person and acts of
Jesus; Mark opens "the beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ".

Concrete and narrative first. Abstraction is spent later, once the viewer has
shown they want it.

---

## Shape

**18 core reels in three arcs of 4 / 6 / 8**, plus an optional finale.
Published as **three playlists**, not one of eighteen.

The asymmetry is deliberate — the on-ramp is short and cheap to finish, and the
arcs lengthen as commitment does:

| Arc | Reels | The viewer is a… |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 | **convert** — who is he, what is God like, who am I, what is wrong with me |
| 2 | 6 | **converted** — what he did, and what it means for me |
| 3 | 8 | **disciple** — how I now live. The abstraction budget exists here, because someone eleven reels in has demonstrated they want this |

Arc 1 at four items reads as almost no commitment. That is the point of an
on-ramp. At two releases a week, the full series is about nine weeks.

### Why not 8, why not 25

- **Against 8:** it forces the collapses that cause the errors — merging
  justification, assurance and sanctification *is* the error the series exists
  to prevent.
- **Against 25:** past roughly twenty, the remaining topics are second-order
  (church government, eschatological timelines, spiritual gifts, canon). They
  are where orthodox believers divide, are least suited to fifty seconds, and
  convert a finishable series into an abandoned one.

The new-believer constraint did not require *fewer* reels. It required
different ones early.

## Arc 1 — यीशु कौन है (Meet him) · reels 1–4

Concrete, narrative, person-first.

| # | Topic | Must land | Must avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | यीशु कौन है? — the person of Christ | Jesus is fully God and fully man, one person — not a guru, avatar or great teacher | Colossians 1:15 "firstborn" left unguarded; "good teacher" framing the audience already accepts |
| 2 | परमेश्वर कैसा है? — God's holiness and goodness, seen in Christ | God is holy and good, and that is the standard everything else is measured against | Collapsing God into "God is love" sentiment — a benevolent uncle who overlooks |
| 3 | आप कौन हैं — made in God's image | Every human bears God's image and has worth God assigns, not society | "Basically good" liberalism; leaving caste-assigned worth unanswered |
| 4 | पाप असल में क्या है — the nature of sin | Sin is the heart's rebellion against God, not merely wrong actions | Therapeutic "mistakes"; karma framing, where sin is an impersonal debit rather than an offence against a person |

**Reel 2 is a reframe, not a new topic.** God's holiness still precedes the
cross — but it is derived from reel 1 rather than delivered as an attribute
list: *look at Jesus and you have seen what God is like* (John 14:9, Heb 1:3),
and what you see is someone holy and good, not indulgent. Isaiah 6 works if you
prefer it narrative; the Christological route is more accessible here.

## Arc 2 — सुसमाचार (The gospel) · reels 5–10

| # | Topic | Must land | Must avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | क्रूस — substitution, judgment folded into the opening | Sin deserves God's judgment, and Christ bore it in our place | Moral influence theory — "he showed how much he loves you" as the whole account |
| 6 | पुनरुत्थान — the resurrection | Jesus rose bodily and historically; this vindicates the cross and secures ours | Spiritualising it into a symbol of new beginnings |
| 7 | मन फिराव और विश्वास — repentance and faith | The response God asks is turning from sin and trusting Christ alone | Decisionism (a prayer, a raised hand, "I gave my life to Jesus"); repentance as penance |
| 8 | विश्वास से धर्मी ठहराया जाना — justification | God declares the believer righteous *now*, by faith, apart from any work | Works-righteousness; infused-rather-than-imputed righteousness; blurring into sanctification |
| 9 | परमेश्वर की सन्तान — adoption | The justified are not merely acquitted but received as sons and daughters | Making adoption a feeling rather than a status |
| 10 | निश्चय — assurance | Assurance rests on Christ's finished work, not on feelings or performance | Blanket assurance with no condition. **Must carry "अगर आप मसीह में हैं"** |

**Judgment is merged, not dropped.** A standalone wrath reel was the highest-risk
item in the series — fifty seconds of judgment with no cross, served cold by the
algorithm, is spiritual abuse. It now opens the cross reel: *this is what the
cross was answering.* It must not become a euphemism: if the fold cannot carry
"sin deserves God's judgment" as a plain sentence, the fold has failed and the
separate reel comes back.

**Assurance moved up into Arc 2.** It is a new believer's most urgent felt
question — *am I actually forgiven?* — and it sits safely downstream of
repentance and justification, so moving it earlier costs nothing doctrinally.
Arc 2 now ends where a new believer most needs to arrive: if someone stops after
ten reels, they stop having heard the gospel and been told their forgiveness is
secure in Christ.

## Arc 3 — मसीह में जीवन (Life in Christ) · reels 11–18

| # | Topic | Must land | Must avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | नया जन्म और पवित्र आत्मा — regeneration and the indwelling Spirit | Every believer has already been given a new heart and the indwelling Spirit — **who is a person, "he", not a force** | Second-blessing / tongues-as-proof framing; the Spirit as power-for-success |
| 12 | पिता, पुत्र, और पवित्र आत्मा — the Trinity, as *naming* | One God, eternally three persons — naming what the viewer has now met in the story | **Every illustration.** Water/ice/steam is modalism; avatar/trimurti is syncretism |
| 13 | परमेश्वर ने बात की है — Scripture as God's word | God has spoken; Scripture is his own word and the final authority | Subjective revelation — dreams, impressions, "God told me" — treated as co-authoritative |
| 14 | प्रार्थना — prayer | Prayer is a child speaking to a Father through Christ, submitted to his will | Word-faith guarantee framing of Luke 11:9-10; transactional vow-making (मन्नत) |
| 15 | पवित्रीकरण — sanctification | Growth is progressive, Spirit-worked, and flows *from* justification, never toward it | Perfectionism; performance religion |
| 16 | आज्ञाकारिता — obedience and love | Obedience is the fruit and evidence of faith, never its ground | Antinomianism and legalism over externals — both, deliberately, in one reel |
| 17 | बपतिस्मा और प्रभु भोज — the ordinances | Commanded signs of a salvation already received, not its cause | Baptismal regeneration; treating either as optional or private |
| 18 | कलीसिया — the local church | A believer cannot follow Jesus alone; the church is the normal context of growth | Nominal attendance mistaken for faith; app-only Christianity — **our own product must be careful not to imply this** |

**Optional finale — reel 19: मसीह का आना.** Christ will return bodily and make
all things new; suffering is not the end of the story. Name no timeline —
rapture and millennium schemes are secondary, divisive, and impossible in fifty
seconds.

**Reels 11 and 12 must stay adjacent and in that order.** Reel 12 works because
the Father, Son and Spirit have all appeared in the narrative by then: the
doctrine *explains the viewer's own experience* rather than introducing an
abstraction. This is why reel 11 must speak of the Spirit as a person — if it
leaves him impersonal, reel 12 has nothing to name.

**Reel 13 lands where the question is finally live:** *how do I hear from God
now?*

## Ordering rules — what will not move

| Rule | What breaks if inverted |
|---|---|
| **2 → 4 → 5** holiness, then sin, then cross | Migration `20260721000001` states the consequence in its own header: teach the crucifixion before God's holiness and justice are established and "the atonement defaults toward moral-influence framing". Reframing to the concrete is fine; moving it after the cross is not |
| **5 before 7** cross before repentance | Reversed, repentance becomes self-reform — the viewer is told to turn from sin with nothing to turn *to* except his own effort. The commonest route by which sincere content produces works-righteousness, and in a culture organised around merit, the likeliest error in the series |
| **7 → 8 → 10** repentance, justification, assurance | The nominal-Christian safeguard, and the reason the series is worth making. Assurance reaching an unrepentant cultural Christian confirms him in exactly the state he needed waking from |
| **8 before 15** justification before sanctification | Reversed, holiness becomes the entry requirement |
| **13 before 14** Scripture before prayer | If prayer is taught before Scripture's authority is established, the vacuum fills with subjective guidance — impressions, dreams, "God told me". Not hypothetical in this context; the repo already deferred 'Discerning God's Will' on exactly this ground |
| **15 and 16 stay separate** | The gap between sanctification and obedience is precisely where antinomianism sits on one side and legalism on the other. A merged reel lands on one of them |
| **Assurance keeps its conditional** | "अगर आप मसीह में हैं", at whatever position. The algorithm still serves it cold to strangers |

### The accepted cost

A new believer's most urgent question is *am I forgiven*, which wants the cross
immediately — and the dependency chain puts it at reel 5. This is a real cost,
accepted rather than resolved, on two grounds: four reels at two a week is about
a fortnight, and a cross arriving before holiness and sin is not the same cross.
The mitigation is that reels 1–4 are the most watchable in the series — a
person, a character, human dignity, a named struggle — so the fortnight is not a
fortnight of throat-clearing.

### Where sin and repentance sit

**Split, deliberately.** Sin's *nature* is diagnostic → reel 4, where God's
holiness has just defined what sin is against. *Repentance* is a gospel response
→ reel 7, after the cross, because repentance preached before the cross has
nowhere to send the person.

### The pilot script

The reworked sin/repentance script ships as the **series trailer**, not as a
numbered reel. It compresses sin, repentance, cross and assurance into fifty
seconds — right for a trailer, wrong for a curriculum. Its content is properly
distributed across reels 4, 5, 7 and 10.

## Alignment with the app's learning paths

The new order **matches** the app's Stage 1 opening — 'Who is Jesus Christ?' is
New Believer Essentials topic 1 — a small confirmation that the person-first
instinct is already in the product.

It **diverges** on the Trinity, which is NBE topic 2 and reel 12 here. The app
should *not* follow the reels: a learning path is entered deliberately and
worked through by someone who has opted in, so the abstraction cost is much
lower, and migration `20260721000006` added it there for a sound reason (the
Trinity being the most-attacked Christian claim in the contexts this app
serves). A reel is served cold to a stranger. Same doctrine, different delivery
risk, different answer — a legitimate divergence, not an inconsistency to fix.

The reels otherwise follow precedent the repo set: migration `20260721000001`
moved baptism after repentance ("inverting Acts 2:38") and the Attributes of God
before the crucifixion. Arc 1 → 2 → 3 is that reasoning on a smaller surface,
and reel 17 keeps baptism downstream of reel 7 for the same Acts 2:38 reason.

### Three places the app looks wrong — open product questions

Flagged, not changed. Recommendation is to fix the app rather than reproduce the
defect, so the marketing matches a corrected product.

1. **Assurance precedes repentance.** 'Confidence in Your Salvation' sits in
   Foundations of Faith at position 3 (`20260119000800_recommended_topics.sql`),
   while 'True Repentance vs. Mere Regret' arrives in
   `20260223000001_new_learning_paths.sql`. The same inversion class the
   migration fixed for baptism, left unfixed one layer up.
2. **Grace precedes sin.** 'Understanding God's Grace' is topic 2 of Rooted in
   Christ, ahead of Sin, Repentance & the Grace of God.
3. **God's holiness is absent from Stage 1.** 'The Attributes of God' is Path
   26 — yet 'What is the Gospel?' is topic 3 of path 1.

### Content gaps with no in-app destination

- **Prosperity teaching** — nearest is 'Grace vs. Works-Based Religion' inside

## Risk register

| Risk | Handling |
|---|---|
| **Reel 5, judgment inside the cross reel** | Must carry "sin deserves God's judgment" as a plain sentence. If it softens into euphemism, split judgment back out as its own reel — and then never publish it before the cross reel is live, pin the cross in the first comment, and build in a forward pointer |
| **Reel 12, Trinity** — every familiar illustration is a heresy; water/ice/steam circulates widely in Indian pulpits (modalism), avatar/trimurti reads as "same as ours" (syncretism) | Make the refusal the content: state the doctrine, then say plainly that the usual pictures break it, and why one of them does |
| **Reel 10, assurance** — a sedative to the unrepentant, and the reel cannot tell who is watching | The conditional "अगर आप मसीह में हैं" survives every editing pass |
| **Reel 11, Spirit** — tongues-as-evidence and second-blessing flashpoints with Pentecostal-background viewers | Teach indwelling positively; explicitly decline to adjudicate cessationism. The most likely topic to derail the series into comment warfare |
| **Reel 8, justification** — Catholic-background viewers | Teach imputed righteousness positively. Do not name Mary, the saints, the Mass or confession — naming converts a lesson into an attack and closes the listener you want |
| **Caste** | No standalone reel. Belongs inside reel 3 (image of God) and reel 18 (one body), taught positively. Fifty seconds cannot carry it and it will be received as political |
| **Prosperity teaching** (Season 2) — arguably the highest-value single reel for this market | Name the teaching, never the teachers. Naming individuals invites defamation exposure and makes the comments about the person |
| **Persecution** (Season 2) — genuine safety implications in several Indian states | No triumphalism, nothing that reads as encouraging confrontation. A reel counselling boldness may reach someone whose risk we cannot assess |
| **Subjective guidance** — cross-cutting | Reel 13 carries it: Scripture as the authority against which impressions are tested. This is why 13 precedes 14 |

### Excluded on purpose

Eschatological timelines · spiritual gifts and cessationism · church government ·
canon formation · predestination/free will · spiritual warfare.

Each is secondary, divisive, or impossible in the format. Predestination is
guaranteed to produce fatalism in fifty seconds. Spiritual warfare is excluded
for the reason the repo already demoted it in Rooted in Christ — "territorial
spirits, deliverance substituted for sanctification".

## Series roadmap — From Believer to Disciple, in five stages

The series is one journey, and its name is Disciplefy's own line: **विश्वासी
से शिष्य तक — From Believer to Disciple.** It is not a Season 1 journey
followed by topic seasons. Each season is a stage of the journey, each arc a
step within the stage, and the spine is the ladder the app already uses —
**seeker → follower → disciple → leader** — because that ladder is a fair
description of what happens to a person who keeps following Jesus. The
ladder and the app's paths are *inputs* here: a checklist of what a believer
needs and a rough order. They are not the structure. The series has its own
shape (short-form, served cold, curiosity-hooked) and is organised by what a
believer actually needs *next*, borrowing from the paths where they help and
ignoring them where they do not.

**83 reels in five stages; about 42 weeks at two a week.** Season 1 is
unchanged above and is stage 1. Each later season has its own curriculum
page; this section fixes the stages, the steps, the viewer at each boundary,
and what each step must establish before the next can land. The per-reel
*must land / must avoid* discipline applies to every topic below.

### A note on the word "disciple"

Season 1's Arc 3 calls its viewer a *disciple*, and the tagline ends at
*disciple*. They are not the same point. Season 1 produces what the ladder
calls a **follower** — someone who has heard the gospel whole and is now
living it in prayer, obedience and a church. The tagline's *disciple* is
Jesus's word in मत्ती 28:19 — one who can stand, speak, and bring others
along — and that is reached at the end of the series, not at reel 18. Season
1's text is left as it is; this roadmap uses the ladder's terms from here.

### The five stages, and why it stops at five

| Stage | Season | Title | Viewer at start → end (ladder) | Arcs | Reels | The step this stage is |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | यीशु कौन है → मसीह में जीवन | new believer → **follower** | 4 / 6 / 8 (+1) | 19 | hearing the gospel whole, in the right order, and starting to live it |
| 2 | 2 | खड़ा होना — standing (the disciple in his own world) | follower → **disciple** who can stand among neighbours, under a pulpit, and at home | 5 / 5 / 6 | 16 | what happens the week after he believes: his family, his church, his life press on him |
| 3 | 3 | बनना — becoming (the disciple's life) | disciple who can stand → **disciple whose character the Spirit is forming**: heart, flesh, and the people around him | 6 / 7 / 8 | 21 | how a disciple lives — the Sermon on the Mount and गलातियों 5 as a life, not a lesson |
| 4 | 4 | वचन और गवाही — the word, and the witness | disciple → **disciple who makes disciples**: reads Scripture for himself and brings one person along | 5 / 5 / 5 | 15 | the disciple of मत्ती 28:19 — the tagline's endpoint |
| 5 | 5 | चरवाहा — the shepherd | discipler → **leader** asked to teach and shepherd, who does it faithfully | 4 / 4 / 4 | 12 | what the church asks next of a disciple who has made one |

**Why not more.** Three reasons, in order of weight. (1) After *leader* there
is no next viewer on the journey to write for — the ladder ends, and so does
the series. (2) What remains after stage 5 is the *excluded on purpose* list
— secondary, divisive, or unsafe served cold — and no later season makes
fifty seconds safer for predestination or polity. (3) A series that ends can
be finished, re-run and pinned as one journey; one that sprawls is abandoned
at reel 40.

**Is 83 finishable?** Yes, narrowly: ten months at two a week, under a year
with slippage. It is the ceiling, not a target, and two cuts have already
been taken to hold it (stage 4's *one story* and *Christ in all Scripture*
merged; stage 5's *canon* reel dropped to the app, which already teaches
'How the Canon Was Formed'). If the run must be shorter, cut in this order,
each a merge that loses framing rather than doctrine: (1) stage 3 *judging
others* into *the tongue*; (2) stage 3 *time and the phone* into *rest and
worship*; (3) stage 4 *testimony* into *the gospel in one breath*; (4) stage
5 *"I don't know"* into *teach the passage*; (5) stage 3 *joy and peace*
into *worry*. That is 78. Below 78 the series stops covering the journey and
should not pretend to.

**Stage boundaries are viewer boundaries, not topic boundaries.** Stage 2
does not begin because stage 1 ran out of doctrine; it begins because the
viewer now goes home to a neighbour. Stage 3 begins because he is staying,
and what forms over the next years is character. Stage 4 begins because he
can stand and live and now needs to speak. Stage 5 begins because someone
has asked him to teach. No season is a prerequisite for the next in the
algorithm's sense — every reel still stands alone — but each is written for
the viewer the previous one produced.

**Where the "Indian context" material went.** It was first drafted as a
season of its own. On the journey framing it is not a theme at all — the
whole series is set in India, and stage 4's *"सब धर्म एक ही"* and stage 3's
*ज्योतिष* are as Indian as anything in stage 2. What the seven produced reels actually are is
**stage 2 of the journey**: the first things that happen to an Indian
believer after he believes are a relative's question, a pulpit's teaching
and a cost at home. So they stay together, as the *standing* stage, rather
than being distributed — distributing them would push the persecution,
festival and family reels either back into stage 1, where the viewer has not
yet got the posture they assume, or forward into stages 3–4, where he has
moved past the need. The produced reels moved between arcs; their content
did not change.

### Stage 3 · Season 3 — बनना (becoming: the disciple's life) · 21 reels

The gap this stage closes: the roadmap was strong on what a believer must
*understand* and thin on how a disciple must *live*. Audited against the
Sermon on the Mount section by section, गलातियों 5:19-23, the New
Testament's *one another* commands, 1 तीमुथियुस 3 / तीतुस 1 read as what
every believer grows toward, the app's Growth and Life & Relationships
topics, and the pilot script's own subject, the outline had no reel for:
the Beatitudes · anger · lust and purity · truthfulness and oaths ·
hypocrisy in religion · worry · judging others · the two builders · joy,
peace, patience, gentleness and self-control as fruit · envy, drunkenness
and sorcery as works of the flesh · forgive / bear / encourage / show
hospitality *one another* · sobriety, gentleness, hospitality, household,
humility and reputation with outsiders from the elder lists · the app's
anxiety, depression, grief, purity, singleness, marriage-when-hard, rest and
Sabbath topics · and the believer who keeps falling into sin, which existed
only as the trailer. Twenty-one reels, three arcs, one stage.

**Why a stage and not an arc or a scattering.** Not an arc: twenty-one
reels do not fit inside a season without breaking the 4–8 rule, and the
material is one subject — the character of a disciple — not a supplement to
standing or witness. Not distributed: a forgiveness reel dropped into stage
2 reaches a viewer whose family is hostile before the persecution brake has
taught him that forgiveness is not a summons to reconcile; a purity reel in
stage 4 reaches a viewer being sent to speak before his life is the
credential. A stage: because on the journey this *is* the step between
standing and speaking. The Sermon on the Mount puts character (मत्ती 5:3-12)
before salt and light (5:13-16) and both before any word about telling
anyone anything; 1 पतरस 2:12 makes the disciple's conduct the thing the
neighbour sees before he hears. Stage 3 sits after stage 2 because standing
is urgent (the week after conversion) and becoming is slow (the years
after), and because several of these reels — forgiveness, truthfulness under
pressure, the horoscope at a wedding — assume the posture toward neighbours
and the tested pulpit that stage 2 fixed.

**Arc 1 — भीतर (the heart: what the Spirit is doing) · 6.** Opens on the
pilot's subject so the whole stage is framed as Spirit-worked growth from
justification, never toward it (Season 1 reel 15 restated in one line in
every reel here).

| # | Topic | Must land | Must avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | गिरता हुआ विश्वासी — the believer who keeps falling | रोमियों 7 is a believer's voice; 1 यूहन्ना 1:9 is for the fallen believer; the Spirit is doing the changing (रोमियों 8:13) and the fall does not undo justification — *अगर आप मसीह में हैं*. The pilot's subject, finally a numbered reel | Perfectionism; licence; *try harder*; conditional absent; the trailer merged into it (they stay separate — the trailer is for a stranger) |
| 2 | परीक्षा — temptation and the way out | God tempts no one (याकूब 1:13-15); desire, not the devil, is where sin is conceived; there is always a way out (1 कुरिन्थियों 10:13); Jesus was tempted and did not sin (इब्रानियों 4:15) | Blaming the devil for one's own desire; warfare framing; a technique |
| 3 | घमंड और नम्रता — pride and humility | *Blessed are the poor in spirit* (मत्ती 5:3); Christ emptied himself (फिलिप्पियों 2:5-8); God opposes the proud (1 पतरस 5:5); *not a recent convert* (1 तीमुथियुस 3:6) is a warning about pride, not age | Self-hatred as humility; false modesty; humility as a technique for promotion |
| 4 | चिंता — worry about tomorrow | मत्ती 6:25-34 read whole: the Father knows, seek the kingdom first, tomorrow has its own trouble; फिलिप्पियों 4:6-7 — distinct from suffering (stage 2), this is fear of what has not happened | Worry shamed as unbelief; denying that anxiety can be an illness needing help; prosperity (*so nothing bad will happen*) |

**Order inside the stage.** Arc 1 first because everything after it is the
Spirit's work in a heart, and the pilot's subject — *I keep falling* — is
where every disciple actually starts this stage. Arc 2 before Arc 3 because
the works of the flesh are named before the *one another* commands in
गलातियों 5–6 itself (5:19-21, then 5:26-6:2), and because a disciple who
has not faced his own anger and tongue cannot bear another's burden. Inside
Arc 3, forgiveness first (everything communal depends on it), family late
(it presupposes the marriage reel of stage 2), rest last (the stage ends on
the shape of a week, as stage 2 ended on the shape of a Monday).

### Stage 4 · Season 4 — वचन और गवाही · 15 reels

The viewer is a disciple who can stand and whose life is being formed;
Season 4 makes him one who can read and speak. **Arc 1 precedes Arc 2
because you cannot share what you cannot read**, and Arc 2 precedes Arc 3
because discipling one person is sharing the gospel with him repeatedly.

**Arc 1 — वचन पढ़ना (reading the Bible well) · 5.** Builds on Season 1 reel
13, which established *that* God has spoken; this arc teaches *how to read
what he said*. (Two topics — *one story* and *Christ in all Scripture* —
merged into one reel to hold the series length; they are the same claim
seen from each end.)

| # | Topic | Must land | Must avoid |

| 1 | एक कहानी, एक नायक — one story, and Christ on every page | Creation, fall, promise, Christ, new creation: one story with one hero (लूका 24:27), by promise, type and pattern the text itself sets up | Moralising Bible characters (*be like David*); allegory (every red cord is the blood); the Old Testament as a different God |
| 2 | हर किताब एक जैसी नहीं — genres | A psalm is not a law, a proverb is not a promise, a letter has an addressee, Revelation is not a newspaper | Reading apocalyptic as timeline; reading Proverbs as guarantee |

| 4 | वादा किसका है? — whose promise is it? | Promises have addressees and covenants; what is *yes* to the church is *yes in Christ* (2 कुरिन्थियों 1:20) | Claiming Israel's land and harvest promises for a bank account; dispensational schemes |

### What was taken from the ladder and the paths, and what was ignored

The paths were read as a checklist of needs (migration `20260721000001`
orders 41 paths seeker → follower → disciple → leader), not as a table of
contents. The series is not mapped onto them one-to-one, and the CTA tables
in each season's curriculum are the only place a reel is tied to a path.

**Taken.** The ladder itself as the spine. The app's person-first opening
(Season 1 reel 1 = NBE topic 1). The follower-level cluster the paths put in
the middle years — suffering, defending the faith, family, money — as the
raw material of stage 2. Bible-reading skill, evangelism and community as
the raw material of stage 4. Leader character and culture as stage 5's. The
app's Life & Relationships and Growth topics — anxiety, depression, grief,
purity, singleness, marriage when hard, rest, contentment — as the checklist
stage 3 was audited against. The
repo's own ordering fixes (`20260721000001`: holiness before the cross,
repentance before baptism) as precedent for every ordering rule.

**Ignored, on purpose.**

- *Path order where it is wrong.* The three Stage 1 inversions flagged
  above (assurance before repentance, grace before sin, holiness absent)
  are not reproduced.
- *Path position where the format disagrees.* The app puts
  'Understanding the Bible' at seeker position 4; the reels put reading
  *well* in stage 4, because reading *at all* was Season 1 reel 13 and
  reading *well* is a disciple's next need, not a seeker's. The app puts
  'Defending Your Faith' at 21; the reels answer the neighbour at the start
  of stage 2, because in India the question arrives before the study does.
  Spiritual warfare is app position 33 and stage 5 here, armour-only.
- *Path topics the format cannot carry.* Polity ('Church Leadership and
  Authority', 'Church Discipline and Restoration') is never a reel; stage 5
  teaches leader character only. Canon ('How the Canon Was Formed') is left
  to the app. Seeker apologetics ('the-big-questions' —
  *Did Jesus exist?*, *Can science disprove God?*) is never a reel; the
  series is for a believer, and a seeker reel served cold is a debate, not a
  lesson. Those lanes belong to the product.
- *Needs the paths do not cover.* Prosperity teaching, discerning God's
  will, festivals and food offered to another, why so many churches — the
  reels have them because the believer needs them next; the app gaps are
  flagged in the Season 2 curriculum for the product to close.

### Excluded on purpose — permanently

Church government · eschatological timelines · predestination and free will
· the fate of those who never heard · Christian political engagement,
conversion law and any current case · comparative religion by name ·
specific healing claims in either direction · **divorce and remarriage** (a
ruling served cold to a stranger in a hard marriage, or a violent one, does
harm in either direction; stage 3's family reel carries safety language and
no ruling) · canon (the app's).

Two items from Season 1's list moved into Season 5 with their refusals
intact: spiritual gifts, spiritual warfare. The rest stay out at every
season for the same reason at every season: secondary, divisive, or unsafe
in fifty seconds served to a stranger. Predestination in particular is
guaranteed to produce fatalism in this format and belongs in the app's
Romans path, where a reader has chapters 1–8 in hand before chapter 9.
