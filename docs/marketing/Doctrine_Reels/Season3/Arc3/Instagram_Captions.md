# Season 3 · Arc 3 — Instagram captions

Short by design: hook, one line, verse, CTA, five hashtags (Instagram's cap).
Hook and closing line are lifted from the reviewed scripts. Pins follow the
script's production notes; under 3·1 and 3·7 the pinned comment is the reel's
own safety line first, the destination beneath it. No comment prompts in this
arc. 3·1 carries its *दूरी पाप नहीं* line and 3·7 its safety section in the
caption itself, because the notes make them uncuttable in every caption. 3·5
takes no cause, caste or justice hashtag. 3·8 is the season finale and the
shared reel, and carries no app, subscription or notification prompt.
