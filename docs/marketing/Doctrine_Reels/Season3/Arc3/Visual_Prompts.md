Same concept, some years on. Rahul is a disciple now — the reels of this
season are about what is happening in him and between him and people, so the
camera stays closer and the scenes are smaller: a floor, a step, a window, a
plate. One real moment per reel, five frames, then the brand outro. Generate
each frame with the previous frame attached. Five clips of 10s, the last one a
HOLD on frame 05 that dissolves into your outro in the edit.

Every prompt here follows the direction guide: each frame names its camera,
set, blocking and light; each clip names its transition type, one camera move,
three action beats, what must not change, and an ambient-only audio bed. Read
the **Staging** note at the top of each reel before generating — it is the map
the prompts are written against.

**Adults only.** Flow rejects anyone under 18, so every person in every frame is an adult.

**Safety rules that override composition** (from the scripts' production
notes): no sin is ever shown or implied on screen — the reels about falling,
temptation and purity show the *after*, never the act, and never a screen,
substance or person that names one. No armour, enemy, battle or warfare
image anywhere. No reunion, embrace, return home or phone call in the
forgiveness reel. No raised hand, bruise, crying woman, man's anger or
wedding footage in the household reel. No before/after and no stock smile in
the grief and joy reels. Nothing in the bribe reel names an office, uniform,
document or scheme — a plain counter and a hand. Never Jesus' face; where a
painting shows him at all it shows hands, a back, a basin.

Five paintings, each on a narrated event: the servant before the king (3·1), the first church breaking bread in a house (3·4), James's own meeting room (3·5), the feet-washing seen from behind with no face (3·7). 3·6 and 3·8 close on the same object from opposite sides — a phone face-down on a step, a phone shut in a drawer — and the season's last frame is a crowd.

## REEL 3·1 — माफ़ किया — पर लौटूँ? · S3·14
A market street. Rahul stops because someone is across the road — the reel never shows who, only a back in the crowd — and his hand tightens on his bag strap. the king and the servant in the painting; Matthew 18 at a chai stall; and Rahul walking away down a different lane, calm, the debt released from a distance. No reunion, no embrace, no call.
**Cast:** Rahul alone. The person is never shown — a back among backs, out of focus, once. · painted scene (the servant before the king) · **Hook text on frame 01:** "आपने माफ़ कर दिया है। पर वह इंसान सामने आए, तो हाथ काँपते हैं।"

along the screen-left side of the frame. The camera stands **on the near

in every frame. The chai stall is at the market's edge: the camera stands in
front of the bench, slightly above, looking down at the Bible on his knee, the
stall's counter behind him. The last frame is a narrow lane leading away from
the market, shot from behind him as he walks away toward the light at its end.
