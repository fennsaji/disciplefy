Same concept, some years on. Rahul is a disciple now — the reels of this
season are about what is happening in him and between him and people, so the
camera stays closer and the scenes are smaller: a floor, a step, a window, a
plate. One real moment per reel, then the brand outro. Generate each frame
with the previous frame attached.

Every prompt here follows the direction guide: each frame names its camera,
set, blocking and light; each clip names its transition type, one camera move,
three action beats, what must not change, and an ambient-only audio bed. Read
the **Staging** note at the top of each reel before generating — it is the map
the prompts are written against.

**Adults only.** Flow rejects anyone under 18, so every person in every frame is an adult.

**Safety rules that override composition** (from the scripts' production
notes): no sin is ever shown or implied on screen — the reels about falling,
temptation and purity show the *after*, never the act, and never a screen,
substance or person that names one. No armour, enemy, battle or warfare
image anywhere. No reunion, embrace, return home or phone call in the
forgiveness reel. No raised hand, bruise, crying woman, man's anger or
wedding footage in the household reel. No before/after and no stock smile in
the grief and joy reels. Nothing in the bribe reel names an office, uniform,
document or scheme — a plain counter and a hand. Never Jesus' face; where a
painting shows him at all it shows hands, a back, a basin.

Three paintings: Jesus at the temple tables seen from behind (2·2), Asaph climbing to the sanctuary (2·6), and the Ephesian believers' bonfire (2·7). 2·3 has no person in any frame, by rule — water and a page. 2·5 never shows alcohol; the whole reel is an evening, a window and a chair.

Timing is unchanged: five clips of 10s, the last one a HOLD on frame 05 that
dissolves into your outro in the edit, the closing line landing over the brand
frame.

## REEL 2·1 — प्रार्थना के लिए ही तो बताया था · S3·07
The Wednesday prayer meeting. A man stands and shares what is happening at home — seen only from behind. Afterwards, at the chai stall, Amit leans in: 'so what's really going on there?' Rahul's mouth stays shut. James 3 on the bus home; and the reel ends with Rahul on the church steps, praying with the man who shared. No whispering, no aunties, no phone thread.
**Cast:** Rahul, Amit, a man from the church (seen only from behind) · **Hook text on frame 01:** "प्रार्थना के लिए ही तो बताया था — उसके घर में क्या चल रहा है।"

**Staging.** Four places, so three clips are CUT-OUTs. In the church hall the
chairs are pulled into a circle in the middle of the floor; the camera stands
**just outside the circle, directly behind the man who stands to share**, and
looks across the circle toward the cream side wall. The man is screen-left of
centre with his back to the camera; Rahul sits across the circle, slightly
screen-right, facing the centre — so he faces the lens. At the chai stall
across the lane from the church gate, the camera stands on the far side of the
lane facing a wooden bench; **Amit is always screen-left, Rahul screen-right**,
both seated facing the lane, the stall's lit counter at the screen-right edge.
On the bus the camera looks down at Rahul's book from in front and above, his
face cropped by the frame top, the window screen-left. On the steps the camera
stands **in the open doorway at the top of the steps looking down over the two
men's backs** to the street; the man is screen-left, Rahul screen-right, both
facing away from the church.

Rahul's wardrobe in every frame: a plain olive-green cotton shirt, sleeves rolled to the forearm. The man: a pale blue checked shirt, greying hair.
