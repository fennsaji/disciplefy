Same concept, some years on. Rahul is a disciple now — the reels of this
season are about what is happening in him and between him and people, so the
camera stays closer and the scenes are smaller: a floor, a step, a window, a
plate. One real moment per reel, five frames a few seconds apart in one
location, then the brand outro. Generate each frame with the previous frame
attached.

Every prompt here follows the direction guide: each frame names its camera,
set, blocking and light; each clip names its transition type, one camera move,
three action beats, what must not change, and an ambient-only audio bed. Read
the **Staging** note at the top of each reel before generating — it is the map
the prompts are written against.

**Adults only.** Flow rejects anyone under 18, so every person in every frame is an adult.

**Safety rules that override composition** (from the scripts' production
notes): no sin is ever shown or implied on screen — the reels about falling,
temptation and purity show the *after*, never the act, and never a screen,
substance or person that names one. No armour, enemy, battle or warfare
image anywhere. No reunion, embrace, return home or phone call in the
forgiveness reel. No raised hand, bruise, crying woman, man's anger or
wedding footage in the household reel. No before/after and no stock smile in
the grief and joy reels. Nothing in the bribe reel names an office, uniform,
document or scheme — a plain counter and a hand. Never Jesus' face; where a
painting shows him at all it shows hands, a back, a basin.

Four paintings in this arc, each where the VO narrates a Bible event: Joseph's door (1·2), the tax collector far off in the temple (1·3), Elijah asleep under the broom tree (1·5), and Paul's chained wrist writing from prison (1·6), seen only from behind. Amit carries 1·5 and returns in 1·6 unchanged, so the arc's joy reel has a sorrowful man in it.

Timing is unchanged: five clips of 10s, the last one a HOLD on frame 05 that
dissolves into your outro in the edit, the closing line landing over the brand
frame.

- **No sin is on screen, ever.** 1·1 and 1·2 show the floor, the stairs — the after, not the act, and nothing that names the act.
