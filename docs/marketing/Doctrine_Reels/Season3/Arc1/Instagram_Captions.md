# Season 3 · Arc 1 — Instagram captions

Short by design: hook, one line, verse, CTA, five hashtags (Instagram's cap).
Hook and closing line are lifted from the reviewed scripts. Pins follow the
script's production notes (1·1 pins *Assurance of Salvation* under the Romans
path; 1·3 pins S3·06; 1·5 pins all three lament paths so the pinned comment
does not diagnose). No comment prompts in this arc: every reel here is about
something the viewer does not say aloud, and 1·5 forbids counsel in the
comments. 1·4's caption never restates मत्ती 6:33 as a provision promise.
