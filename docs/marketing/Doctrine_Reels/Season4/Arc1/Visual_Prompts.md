Arjun and Rahul's uncle: (reference sheets below)

Same concept. Rahul now reads the Bible for himself, speaks when asked, and
walks with one younger believer — Arjun. One real moment per reel, five
frames a few seconds apart, then the brand outro. Generate each frame with
the previous frame attached.

Every prompt here follows the direction guide: each frame names its camera,
set, blocking and light; each clip names its transition type, one camera move,
three action beats, what must not change, and an ambient-only audio bed. Read
the **Staging** note at the top of each reel before generating — it is the map
the prompts are written against.

**Adults only.** Flow rejects anyone under 18, so every person in every frame is an adult.

**Rules that override composition** (from the scripts' production notes): a
Bible on screen is always a printed Bible — the reading never happens on a
phone. No street, stage, crowd, microphone, leaflet or door-to-door scene. No
other faith's place of worship, symbol, text, festival or dress, ever. No
food of any kind in the law reel; no property, plot or money in the promise
reel; no stones or sling in the David reel. No count of anything. The app,
its discipler feature and its groups never appear. Never Jesus' face; where
he is in a painting he is a distant figure from behind.

Three paintings, each where the VO narrates a Bible event: Israel's trembling ranks with one figure walking out (1·1, no weapon shown), the exiles building houses by the river (1·3), Joshua looking over the Jordan (1·4). Every frame that shows reading shows a printed Bible.

Timing is unchanged: five clips of 10s, the last one a HOLD on frame 05 that
dissolves into your outro in the edit, the closing line landing over the brand
frame.

## REEL 1·1 — एक कहानी, एक नायक · S4·01
Rahul's Sunday Bible class for young adults. A lanky classmate flexes like a giant-slayer — 'I'm David!' — and Rahul laughs, then isn't sure. The painting is the trembling ranks and one figure walking out. At his desk he turns the Bible from its first page to its last. The reel ends with the class on the floor around one open Bible. No stones, no sling, no red cord anywhere.
**Cast:** Rahul, a young-adult Sunday class (men and women in their twenties), Rohit (one-off — the lanky classmate) · painted scene (Israel's ranks on the hillside; one young figure walking out, from behind) · **Hook text on frame 01:** "बचपन से सुना — दाऊद जैसे बनो, गोलियत गिराओ।"

**Staging.** A small church classroom with a window on the left wall and the
door behind the camera. The class sits on floor mats in a loose ring. In the
classroom the camera stands **just inside the door looking across the room
toward the window wall**: Rahul sits on the floor **screen-right**, his back to
the right wall, facing screen-left into the ring; the class sits **screen-left**
facing him, so they are in profile or three-quarter. Rohit stands in the
middle of the ring facing Rahul (screen-right). At night Rahul's desk faces a
plain wall; the camera stands **in front of the desk and above the book**,
looking down, Rahul's face cropped by the frame top. The last frame looks
straight down on the classroom floor; Rahul keeps the screen-right side of the
ring.

- **Every person is an adult.** 1·1's Sunday class is a young-adult class (Rohit, the "I'm David" classmate, is about 22); 1·3's exiles are men and women at work.
- **1·1 has no stones, sling or red cord** — only the ranks, one figure walking out, and an open Bible.
- **1·2 never shows the one who wandered** — a grown man — and never hints at his return. The VO's "बच्चे … लड़के … वह बच्चा" stays as written; the picture is only his empty room and Sunil.
- **1·3's verse card is never readable** and never on screen alone as a quote; the chapter is always read with it.
- **1·4 shows no property, plot, sign, keys or money** — feet on a path, then Joshua 1.
- **1·5 shows no food of any kind.** Thread and cloth only.
- **Arjun** (reference above, used in Arcs 2–3) is 23 with a fuller short beard so he never reads as under-age.
- **The five closing questions** are the reels' last spoken lines; none is cut from any clip.
- **Pins** (from the arc's production notes): follow the script file.
