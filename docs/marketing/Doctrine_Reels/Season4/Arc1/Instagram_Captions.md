# Season 4 · Arc 1 — Instagram captions

Short by design: hook, one line, verse, CTA, five hashtags (Instagram's cap).
Hook and closing line are lifted from the reviewed scripts. Every caption
carries the reel's closing question — the notes make all five uncuttable.
No caption names a preacher, church, channel or movement as the misreader, and
none adds a technique, a course or a book. 1·3 never prints यिर्मयाह 29:11 or
फिलिप्पियों 4:13 alone and never links the memory-verse feature. Pins follow
the script's production notes.
