Same concept. Rahul now reads the Bible for himself, speaks when asked, and
walks with one younger believer — Arjun, a man of 23. One real moment per
reel, five frames a few seconds apart, then the brand outro. Generate each
frame with the previous frame attached.

Every prompt here follows the direction guide: each frame names its camera,
set, blocking and light; each clip names its transition type, one camera move,
three action beats, what must not change, and an ambient-only audio bed. Read
the **Staging** note at the top of each reel before generating — it is the map
the prompts are written against.

**Adults only.** Flow rejects anyone under 18, so every person in every frame is an adult.

Timing is unchanged: five clips of 10s, the last one a HOLD on frame 05 that
dissolves into your outro in the edit, the closing line landing over the brand
frame.

**Rules that override composition** (from the scripts' production notes): a
Bible on screen is always a printed Bible — the reading never happens on a
phone. No street, stage, crowd, microphone, leaflet or door-to-door scene. No
other faith's place of worship, symbol, text, festival or dress, ever. No
food of any kind in the law reel; no property, plot or money in the promise
reel; no stones or sling in the David reel. No count of anything. The app,
its discipler feature and its groups never appear. Never Jesus' face; where
he is in a painting he is a distant figure from behind or a silhouette in light.

Paintings: the eleven on the mountain (3·1, Jesus a distant figure in light), Philip running up to the chariot (3·2), David over the scroll (3·3), the nets being mended (3·4), the father running (3·5). Arjun enters in 3·1, reads in 3·2 and is absent in 3·5 — and is never shown leaving: no clip ends on Arjun walking away alone.

## REEL 3·1 — कुछ को शक था · S4·11
The eleven on the mountain in Galilee — some kneeling, some hanging back. Then Sunday: Rahul notices Arjun, new, alone on the back bench. He sits beside him with two teas. Weeks later they walk the church courtyard together; and the reel ends in Rahul's room with one printed Bible between them.
**Cast:** Rahul, Arjun · painted scene (the eleven on the mountain — Jesus a distant figure in light) · **Hook text on frame 01:** "यीशु की आख़िरी आज्ञा — जाओ, चेले बनाओ।"

**Staging.** In the painting the viewer stands low on the slope behind the
eleven, who face up the hill toward a distant figure standing in the morning
light. Inside the church the camera stands **at the front of the hall beside
the front bench, looking back down the centre aisle toward the open back
door**: the benches face the front, so anyone seated faces the lens, and the
people leaving walk away from us toward the door. **Arjun is always
screen-left, Rahul screen-right** — on the bench, in the courtyard and on the
floor of Rahul's room. In the courtyard the camera follows from behind and to
the right. In Rahul's room the camera sits on the floor in front of them,
facing the bed they lean against.
