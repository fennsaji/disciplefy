# Season 4 · Arc 3 — Instagram captions

Short by design: hook, one line, verse, CTA, five hashtags (Instagram's cap).
Hook and closing line are lifted from the reviewed scripts, with each reel's
caption-uncuttables. **No caption names, shows or links the app's discipler
feature, mentors or fellowship groups** — these reels say the app is not the
church and does not make disciples. 3·2 carries that line whole. 3·4 carries
the safety line. 3·5 carries no *backslider* word and no verdict. Pins follow
the notes (under 3·3 and 3·4 the pinned comment is the reel's own line first).
