# Disciplefy — Instagram Launch & How-To Carousels

Ready-to-build carousel scripts (slide-by-slide) for the launch announcement and
the four core how-to guides. Follow the Brand Bible voice and the Brand Visual System.

## Guardrails (apply to every slide)
- **Voice:** encouraging, Christ-centered, humble, hope-filled, practical. **Never** clickbait, hype, fear-based.
- **Visual:** indigo/cream background, **gold accent** (verse/keyword in gold), Poppins headings, minimal — one idea per slide. Same cover template + same final CTA card across all posts so the set reads as one series.
- **Hook in the first ~6 words** of every cover (that's what stops the carousel scroll).
- **How-to step slides use a real app screenshot** in a clean device frame.
- **Bilingual:** carousels are the bilingual-friendly format. Where marked *(+ Hindi)*, add the Hindi line under the English on that slide.

## Rollout & consistency
1. **Post 1 (Launch)** first — the big moment / payoff to "Coming Soon."
2. Then one how-to per week: **Generate Study → Learning Paths → Memory Verses → Voice Discipler** (mirrors the app's core loop).
3. **Same cover template + gold accent + device-framed screenshots** across all five → one recognizable branded series.
4. **Every last slide is the identical CTA card** ("Start free → link in bio").
5. Amplify each carousel in **Stories** (English-led, with a Hindi sticker/teaser to seed the Saturday Hindi audience).

## Theology-safe note
Voice Discipler copy must always frame it as a **companion that points back to Scripture and community — never a replacement** for pastor, church, or the Bible.
