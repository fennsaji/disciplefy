## Production Workflow
Each week, produce the **following** week's content (rolling model, matches the Operating System):
| Task | Timing |
|---|---|
| Review last week's KPIs, plan next week, finalize scripts | Early in the week |
| Generate Google Flow / Veo clips | Before voice-over |
| Record voice-overs | — |
| Edit Reels, design Carousels (incl. that week's Sunday recap) | — |
| Final review, schedule all 7 posts | Before the new week starts |
| Reply to comments/DMs | Daily, ongoing |

Also track separately (not on this content calendar): **2 meaningful product improvements** for the month — assign to specific weeks in the product backlog.

## Success Metrics
Track weekly: Reel Reach · Average Watch Time · Saves · Shares · Profile Visits · Website Clicks · App Installs
Ignore likes. Optimize for: Saves · Shares · Retention · Installs
*(Canonical KPI list: `Disciplefy Operating System` → KPI Dashboard.)*

## Content Principles
Every piece of content must:
- Be faithful to Scripture.
- Teach one clear biblical truth.
- Give one practical next step.
- Encourage spiritual growth.
- Point people toward Jesus.
- Naturally introduce Disciplefy as a tool for deeper Bible study — not as the hero.

**Mission First. Content Second. App Third.**
