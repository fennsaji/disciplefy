# Disciplefy Operating System (Disciplefy OS)
Version: 1.1 *(updated — see Changelog at bottom)*

## Mission
Helping believers become true disciples of Jesus through God's Word.
Every decision, feature, piece of content, and conversation should move people one step closer to Jesus.

## The Disciplefy Flywheel
Mission → Create Biblical Content → Reach New People → Build Trust → Join Community → Install Disciplefy → Complete Bible Studies → Spiritual Growth → Invite Others → Repeat

## Core Principles
1. **Mission Before Metrics** — we care about changed lives more than viral videos. Views are helpful. Transformation is the goal.
2. **Bible First** — every piece of content must be rooted in Scripture. Never create content simply because it is trending.
3. **Practical Faith** — every study and every Reel should answer: "What should I do today because of God's Word?"
4. **Excellence Over Quantity** — one excellent Reel is better than five average ones.
5. **Build Trust First** — never force app downloads. Provide value first.

## Weekly Operating Rhythm
This rhythm produces content **for the following week's calendar** — since the calendar now publishes daily (see `Disciplefy Content Calendar`), nothing here can be scripted/shot/edited/published within the same week and still go out on time. Read each day's tasks as "prepping next week."

| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| Monday | Planning — review analytics, plan next week's content, choose Bible passages, assign tasks, product meeting |
| Tuesday | Writing — Reel scripts, captions, carousel content, Story ideas |
| Wednesday | Production — generate Google Flow/Veo clips, record talking-head videos, voice-overs, collect B-roll |
| Thursday | Editing — edit videos, thumbnails, review captions, schedule next week's posts |
| Friday | Publishing — publish today's content, reply to comments/DMs, share to Stories |
| Saturday | Community **+** today's scheduled product-demo Reel — prayer requests, answer questions, share testimonies, ask for feedback (dual-purpose day: publish *and* engage) |
| Sunday | Content publishes (see calendar) **and** personal reflection — attend church, take notes, find new content ideas, review the week, pray for Disciplefy |

**Note:** Sunday now carries a light, pre-built post (Sunday Reflection carousel) — it is *not* a no-content day anymore. Keep Sunday's post production-light so church/worship time stays protected; do the actual reflection/planning work spread across the week, not crammed into Sunday.

## Decision Framework
Before doing anything, ask:
- Does this help believers become disciples?
- Is it faithful to Scripture?
- Will this genuinely help someone?
- Is this excellent?
- Would we be proud of this one year from now?

If any answer is No, don't proceed.

## Monthly Review Questions
- What content helped people most?
- What generated the most meaningful conversations?
- What product features increased Bible study?
- What confused users?
- What should we stop doing?
- What should we improve?

## Quarterly Objectives
Grow in: Biblical depth · Product quality · Community engagement · User retention · Partnerships. Not just follower count.

## Success Definition
Disciplefy succeeds when people say: **"I know God better because of Disciplefy."**
Not: "I watched a nice Reel."

## Daily Checklist
**Product** — Review user feedback · Fix important issues · Improve one small thing
**Content** — Publish or schedule content · Reply to comments · Reply to DMs
**Community** — Pray for users · Encourage someone · Share one Bible truth
**Growth** — Review analytics · Learn one new marketing insight · Record future content ideas

## Annual North Star
Disciplefy is not trying to become the biggest Christian content page. Disciplefy exists to become the most trusted discipleship platform for believers — helping people understand God's Word, follow Jesus faithfully, and make disciples who make disciples. Everything we build should move people one step closer to becoming like Christ.
