# Disciplefy Growth Bible
Version: 1.1 *(updated — see Changelog)*

## Purpose
This document defines how Disciplefy grows its audience, community, and app users.

## Primary Goals
Build trust · Build community · Grow the Disciplefy brand · Increase app installs · Create lifelong disciples

## Annual Goals
- 50,000 Instagram followers
- 10,000 App installs
- 1,000 Active community members

*These have no interim milestones — hard to tell mid-year if you're on track. Recommend breaking into quarterly checkpoints once Month 1 data comes in (e.g. Q1 target ≈ 15-20% of annual), rather than only measuring against the full year.*

## Content Targets
- 4 Reels per week
- 2 Carousels per week *(updated from 1 — matches the actual Content Calendar cadence: Tue "Bible Tips" + Thu "Bible Study" carousels. 2026 Instagram data shows carousels have the highest engagement rate of any format, so this is a deliberate raise, not scope creep — see `Disciplefy Operating System` Changelog.)*
- Daily Stories (2x/day: Morning + Evening — see `Content Playbook` for the 2026 rationale on why 2x beats 4x)
- 1 Live session per month — now assigned to Week 2 in `Content Calendar — Month 1`, combined with the monthly Q&A

## Daily Growth Routine
Reply to every comment. Reply to every DM. Engage with Christian creators. Pray for the community. Review analytics.

## Weekly Review
Track: Views · Reach · Watch Time · Shares · Saves · Profile Visits · Website Clicks · App Installs
*(Full canonical KPI list lives in `Disciplefy Operating System` → KPI Dashboard — reference that instead of maintaining a second list here.)*

## Monthly Experiments
Test new hooks. Test new CTAs. Try collaboration posts. Try different posting times. Experiment with thumbnails.

## Collaboration Strategy
Partner with: Christian creators · Youth pastors · Churches · Worship leaders · Bible teachers · Christian podcasts

## Community Strategy
Prayer requests · Bible reading challenges · Monthly Q&A · Live Bible studies · User testimonies · Feature requests
*These need calendar slots too — same gap as the OS's monthly community goals (Bible Challenge / Q&A / Testimony Campaign). Assign specific weeks so they actually happen.*

## Paid Promotion
No paid-ad plan existed anywhere in this doc before, despite one already being written elsewhere. The Saturday "Disciplefy Stories" series (`Daily Devotion/Saturday — Disciplefy Stories.md`) has 6 episodes flagged as **Hero Episodes** — pre-selected as the strongest candidates to boost/run as ads once there's budget for it (Ep1 "How Do You Study the Bible?", Ep2 "Where Should I Start?", Ep5 "Google or the Bible?", Ep9 "I Can Never Remember Bible Verses", Ep13 "Can I Ask a Question?", Ep17 "This Isn't Another Bible App"). Use that list as the starting point whenever ad spend opens up, rather than picking new episodes to boost ad hoc.

## Success Metrics
Followers · Engagement Rate · Retention · Website Clicks · App Installs · Daily Active Users · Study Completion Rate

## Quarterly Questions
- Are we helping believers become disciples?
- Is our content changing lives?
- What content is producing the most spiritual impact?
- What should we stop doing?
- What should we improve next quarter?
