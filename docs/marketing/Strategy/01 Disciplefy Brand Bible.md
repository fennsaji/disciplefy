# Disciplefy Brand Bible

## Purpose
This document defines who Disciplefy is and why it exists. Every decision about the product, content, design, and marketing should align with this document.

## Mission
Helping believers become true disciples of Jesus through God's Word.

## Vision
To see believers across the world grow into mature disciples who know God deeply and follow Jesus faithfully.

## Tagline
From Believer to Disciple.

## Brand Promise
Disciplefy helps believers understand God's Word, grow spiritually, and follow Jesus every day.

## Brand Values
- 📖 **Biblical** — we stay true to God's Word.
- ❤️ **Practical** — we help people live what they learn.
- 🌱 **Growth** — we help believers become more like Jesus.

## Target Audience
**Primary** — Christians aged 18–35 · Young professionals · College students · Believers who want to grow spiritually
**Secondary** — Church leaders · Small groups · New believers

## Brand Personality
Disciplefy is: Wise · Encouraging · Practical · Humble · Hope-filled · Christ-centered
Never: Condemning · Clickbait · Political · Divisive · Fear-based

## Core Message
Knowing Jesus is the beginning. Becoming like Him is the journey. Disciplefy exists to help believers grow into true disciples.

## Elevator Pitch
Disciplefy is a discipleship platform that helps believers understand the Bible, apply it to everyday life, and grow closer to Jesus.

## Instagram Bio
Helping believers become disciples of Jesus. 📖
Bible Study • Spiritual Growth
👇 Start your journey

## Success Statement
When someone hears "Disciplefy," they should immediately think: **"This is where I grow closer to Jesus."**

## Decision Filter
Before launching anything, ask:
- Is it biblical?
- Is it practical?
- Will it help someone become more like Jesus?
- Does it align with our mission?
