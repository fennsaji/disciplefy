# Disciplefy Content Bible
Version: 1.1 *(updated — see Changelog)*

## Purpose
This document defines how Disciplefy creates educational, engaging, and Christ-centered content.

## Content Pillars
1. **Bible Study** — understanding Scripture in context.
2. **Practical Discipleship** — living out biblical truth.
3. **Spiritual Growth** — prayer, habits, character, obedience.
4. **Encouragement** — hope from God's Word.
5. **Disciplefy** — features, tutorials, updates, testimonials.

## Reel Formula
Hook (0–3 sec) → Problem → Bible Truth → Practical Application → Call to Action

## Hooks
- Most Christians miss this...
- You're reading this verse wrong...
- Here's what Jesus actually meant...
- Before you read your Bible tomorrow...
- If you're struggling with prayer...

## Call to Action
Rotate these:
- Save this for later.
- Share with a friend.
- What's your biggest takeaway?
- Read this chapter today.
- Try this study in Disciplefy.

## Story Content
Daily Verse · Prayer Box · Bible Quiz · Poll · Behind the Scenes · Founder Update · Church · Community Questions

*Story cadence: 2x/day (Morning + Evening) is the current standard — daily presence is what the algorithm rewards, not touch-count. See `Content Playbook — Week 1` for the 2026 rationale.*

## Caption Structure
Hook → Short explanation → Application → Question → CTA

## Content Rules
Every post should:
- Teach something biblical.
- Give one practical takeaway.
- Be simple to understand.
- Encourage action.
- Point people toward Jesus, not ourselves.
