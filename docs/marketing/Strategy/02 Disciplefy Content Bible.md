# Disciplefy Content Bible
Version: 1.1 *(updated — see Changelog)*

## Purpose
This document defines how Disciplefy creates educational, engaging, and Christ-centered content.

## Content Pillars
1. **Bible Study** — understanding Scripture in context.
2. **Practical Discipleship** — living out biblical truth.
3. **Spiritual Growth** — prayer, habits, character, obedience.
4. **Encouragement** — hope from God's Word.
5. **Disciplefy** — features, tutorials, updates, testimonials.

## Reel Formula
Hook (0–3 sec) → Problem → Bible Truth → Practical Application → Call to Action

## Hooks
- Most Christians miss this...
- You're reading this verse wrong...
- Here's what Jesus actually meant...
- Before you read your Bible tomorrow...
- If you're struggling with prayer...

## Call to Action
Rotate these:
- Save this for later.
- Share with a friend.
- What's your biggest takeaway?
- Read this chapter today.
- Try this study in Disciplefy.

## Content Rules
Every post should:
- Teach something biblical.
- Give one practical takeaway.
- Be simple to understand.
- Encourage action.
- Point people toward Jesus, not ourselves.

### Verse Variety Rule
One verse = one post. Never assign a Bible verse that any other post, reel, carousel, story, or message in the same season already uses — repeated verses make the feed feel recycled and narrow the range of Scripture we put in front of people.

- **Before assigning a verse**, search the season's folders for it:
  `grep -rn "Nehemiah 8:8" docs/marketing/Content_Playbook_Season1/ marketing/Content_Playbook_Season1/`
- **Within one post is fine.** The same day's caption, image prompt, and story slot naturally repeat that post's verse — the rule applies *across* posts, not inside one.
- **Allowed exceptions** (must be deliberate and noted in the doc where the reuse happens):
  - A season theme verse explicitly designated at season start (Season 1 has none).
  - In-app demo typing examples in screen recordings (e.g. typing "Romans 8:28" into Generate) — these demonstrate UI, not devotional content. Still vary them when convenient.
  - Translations of the same piece (e.g. Hindi version of an English message) share their source's verses by definition.

**Known Season 1 repeats (pre-rule; do not extend the pattern):** Hebrews 10:24-25 (Sunday Reflection in all 4 weeks), Psalm 119:105 and Hebrews 12:1 (PreWeek1 Bridge duplicates Week 1's posts), Nehemiah 8:8 (Week 1 Monday, Week 4, and the Need-for-Disciplefy message), Acts 17:11 (Week 3 + message), 2 Timothy 2:15 (Week 2 + message), Romans 8:28 (demo verse in PreWeek1, Week 1, and the One-Minute Reel).
