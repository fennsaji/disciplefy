# Disciplefy — Carousel Image-Generation Prompts (ChatGPT / DALL·E / GPT-4o)

Image prompts to generate each slide of the five Instagram carousels in
`Instagram_Launch_and_HowTo_Carousels.md`. **Light / warm theme** — matches the
approved reference cover (bright cream, natural window light, warm wood, cozy props,
dark bold headline, gold logo & accents).

## How to use
1. **Paste the STYLE BLOCK first, then the slide prompt** — keeps all slides in a carousel visually consistent (critical).
2. Generate a carousel's slides **in one session**; for later slides add *"match the exact style, palette, lighting, and typography of the previous image."*
3. ⚠️ **AI text is unreliable.** For crisp on-brand type, generate slides **text-free** (drop the "render text" line) and overlay the real text in Canva using Poppins. If letting the model render text, keep it short and expect a few regenerations.
4. **How-to step slides need REAL app screenshots** — use the "device-mockup staging" prompt to make the branded frame, then composite your actual screenshot in and add the step caption in Canva.
5. **Only slide 1 (the cover) gets the "Swipe →" cue**; every slide carries the brand frame, but the swipe arrow belongs on the cover only.

## 🎨 STYLE BLOCK (prepend to EVERY prompt)
> Premium, warm, bright slide designed as **one frame in a multi-slide, swipeable Instagram carousel** for a Christian discipleship app called **Disciplefy**. Portrait **4:5 (1080×1350)**. **Light warm theme:** soft **cream / off-white background** (`#FAF8F5`) bathed in **bright natural window light** — airy, cozy, inviting. Scene styled on a **warm wooden desk** with tasteful real props (an **open Bible**, a **ceramic coffee mug**, a small **potted plant / olive branch**, a **leather journal with a pen**), softly sunlit from a window with gentle shadows and subtle light bloom. **Bold near-black charcoal** (`#1E1E1E`) headline in a clean modern sans-serif (Poppins style), left-aligned; short subline in warm gray. **Gold accents** (`#B8860B` / `#C79A3B`) for the logo, icons, dividers, and key words.
>
> **Brand frame on every slide:** top-left, the **gold Disciplefy logo** (an open-book-with-cross icon + "Disciplefy" wordmark) with a **thin gold divider + a small gold sparkle** beneath it; bottom-left, a **small gold globe icon + "disciplefy.in"** followed by a long thin gold divider line. Generous negative space, one clear focal message.
>
> Mood: warm, hopeful, reverent, high-end, cozy. **No** dark/moody look, clutter, cheesy stock, distorted/garbled text, watermarks, or literal clip-art crosses.

## Consistency checklist
- **Every slide is light + warm** — cream background, natural window light, warm wood, cozy props. No dark/moody frames.
- **Brand frame on every slide:** gold Disciplefy logo + divider top-left; gold globe + "disciplefy.in" + gold line bottom-left.
- **Headlines** are bold charcoal, left-aligned, big; **gold reserved for the key word** (verse, "LIVE", "Free", "Disciple") and all icons/dividers.
- Same **gold "Step N" badge** + device-frame across all how-tos → the five carousels read as one branded series.
- **Cover (1)** carries the "Swipe →" cue; the rest do not.
