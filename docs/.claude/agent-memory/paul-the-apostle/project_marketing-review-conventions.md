Disciplefy marketing/ministry copy (in `marketing/Content_Playbook_Season1/`) quotes Scripture in NIV wording; reviews must verify quotes against NIV specifically, not ESV/KJV (e.g., Acts 8:31 NIV is "explains it to me," not "guides me"; Hos 4:6 NIV is "from lack," not "for lack").

**Why:** User explicitly stated NIV-style wording is the standard for this content series and asked for translation-accuracy checks as a review criterion (2026-07-14 review of Need_For_Disciplefy_Message.md).

**How to apply:** In every marketing-content review, check each quote against NIV wording and flag silent translation mixing. Also always verify the copy never positions the app as replacing the Holy Spirit, the local church, or human discipleship — recommend an explicit "alongside the church, not replacing it" clause when the app is framed as "the guide" (esp. Acts 8 framing).
