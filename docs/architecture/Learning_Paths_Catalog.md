## History worth knowing

### The UUID collision (2026-03-13 → 2026-03-16)

`20260313000001_new_learning_paths_v2.sql` tried to add five paths reusing IDs `…026`–`…030`. Four of those IDs already belonged to paths created three weeks earlier.

`ON CONFLICT (id) DO NOTHING` silently skipped the `learning_paths` inserts — but the accompanying `learning_path_topics` inserts still ran, attaching the new topics to the *wrong existing paths*. Only `…030` (Hebrews) landed cleanly, its ID being unused.

`20260316000002_fix_learning_paths_uuid_collision.sql` repaired this: it deleted the misplaced topic rows and recreated Jesus's Parables, Sermon on the Mount, Romans, and Crucifixion & Resurrection at fresh IDs `…031`–`…034`. Paths `…026`–`…029` kept their original content.

Three further collision fixes followed on 2026-03-27 (`20260327000003/4/5`), relinking topic IDs between Crucifixion/Work & Vocation, Hebrews/Historical Reliability, and Parables & Sermon on the Mount/Spiritual Warfare & The Local Church. Those touched topics only — no path rows or counts changed.

**Lesson for future seed migrations:** `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` on a parent insert while child inserts proceed unguarded produces silent data corruption. Guard the child rows on the parent actually having been inserted, or use IDs generated at insert time rather than hardcoded literals.

### Other structural changes

- `20260223000001_new_learning_paths.sql` added 19 paths. Note that "The Big Questions" is appended as a Part 5 addendum *after* the migration's own verification block — easy to miss when reading the file.
- `20260413000002_fix_learning_paths_review_issues.sql` removed a duplicate "Responding to Common Questions" topic from Christianity & Culture (7 → 6).
