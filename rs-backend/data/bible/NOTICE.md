references in the Hindi IRV), Strong's numbers, headings, Psalm titles and book
introductions were removed. Format: `#name<TAB>USFM<TAB>book name` rows, then
`USFM<TAB>chapter<TAB>verse<TAB>text` (verse may be a merged span like `24-25`).
